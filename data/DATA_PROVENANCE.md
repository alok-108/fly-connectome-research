# Connectome Data Provenance & Scientific Lineage

This document establishes the scientific lineage, legal licensing, and technical provenance of the Drosophila connectome datasets evaluated and utilized in this project.

The project strictly distinguishes between:
1. **AUTHORITATIVE BIOLOGICAL DATASETS**: The primary reconstructions generated by electron microscopy (EM), segmentation, and proofreading consortia (HHMI Janelia, Princeton Seung Lab, MRC LMB, Cambridge).
2. **THIRD-PARTY MIRRORS / EXPORTS**: Curated static network archives (e.g., Netzschleuder, Zenodo) packaging the connectome for network science benchmarks.
3. **OUR PROCESSED DATA**: Standardized, memory-optimized Parquet tables in `data/processed/` filtered strictly for local computation and virtual lesion experiments.

## 2. Ingestion & Preprocessing Steps for Local Project

For the local experimental framework on an Intel Core i5-1235U with 16 GB RAM:

1. **Source Download**:
   - Archive: `fly_hemibrain.csv.zip` (16.1 MiB)
   - Mirror: Netzschleuder network catalogue (maintained by Dr. Tiago Peixoto, verified mirror of Janelia FlyEM Hemibrain v1.2.1).
   - Ingestion date: 2026-09-28.
   - Raw destination: `data/raw/fly_hemibrain.csv.zip` (immutable, excluded from Git).

2. **Integrity Validation**:
   - Validates existence, byte size (~16.86 MB), and presence of `nodes.csv` (21,739 rows) and `edges.csv` (4,259,624 rows).
   - Verifies header format, zero missing indices, and non-empty weights.

3. **Memory-Safe Parquet Conversion**:
   - `nodes.csv` $\longrightarrow$ `data/processed/neurons.parquet`:
     - Columns: `neuron_id` (int64, mapped from contiguous index), `body_id` (int64, Janelia anatomical ID), `cell_type` (string, morphologic class), `instance` (string), `neuropil` (string).
   - `edges.csv` $\longrightarrow$ `data/processed/connections.parquet`:
     - Chunked streaming via `pyarrow.parquet.ParquetWriter` (100,000 rows/chunk).
     - Peak RAM during conversion: **< 30 MB**.
     - Columns: `source` (uint32), `target` (uint32), `weight` (uint16, synapse count), `roi` (categorical neuropil).

4. **Zero-Invention Guarantee**:
   - No neuron IDs, synapse counts, or cell-type annotations are generated, interpolated, or fabricated.
   - All edge weights represent real electron-microscopy reconstructed synaptic counts from the Janelia FlyEM team.

## 3. Disclaimers & Limitations

- **Central Brain Representation**: The hemibrain dataset covers ~half of the central Drosophila brain. Contralateral projections extending beyond the cropped volume are truncated as documented in Scheffer et al. (2020).
- **Graph vs Biological Circuit**: In this computational framework, the graph $G = (V, E)$ represents structural synaptic connectivity. Functional connectivity, electrical gap junctions, modulatory neuropeptides, and non-synaptic signaling are not captured in the raw synapse count.
- **Model vs Biology**: Graph topology analyses reflect anatomical EM reconstructions. Computational simulations (e.g. LIF) are simplified phenomenological models and must not be interpreted as full biological fly cognition or behavior.
